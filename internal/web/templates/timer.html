Checks that need someone at the track

These cannot be automated — someone has to open a gate or roll a car down the track.

Start gate

Press start, then open and close the gate. The most common race-night failure, and this also measures how long the switch bounces.

Lane mapping

Roll a car down the lane you pick. If the timer reports a different lane, the wiring is reversed — worth knowing before a whole race is recorded backwards.

Test heat

Runs a full practice heat: arm, stage, release, collect. The results are discarded and never touch a race.
